Chicken Tikka Masala

Description

Chicken Tikka Masala is a rich and flavorful Indian-inspired dish that has become a beloved favorite around the world. It features tender chunks of marinated chicken, traditionally cooked in a tandoor or grilled, then simmered in a creamy tomato-based sauce infused with aromatic spices like garam masala, cumin, and turmeric. The result is a savory, slightly smoky curry with a perfect balance of heat and sweetness. Often served over steamed basmati rice or alongside warm naan bread, Chicken Tikka Masala is a comforting and satisfying meal. While its exact origins are debated—some credit British chefs for its creation—the dish beautifully blends South Asian spices with Western preferences, making it a staple in Indian restaurants globally.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoons garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, grated
  • 1 can (15 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup heavy cream or coconut milk
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • Cooked basmati rice or naan (for serving)

Cooking Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine yogurt, lemon juice, garam masala, cumin, coriander, turmeric, cayenne pepper, and salt.
  2. Mix well to create a marinade.
  3. Add chicken pieces and marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight in the fridge).
  4. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  5. Add minced garlic and grated ginger; cook for another minute until fragrant.
  6. Add marinated chicken to the skillet and cook until browned on all sides.
  7. Stir in crushed tomatoes and bring to a simmer. Cook for about 10-15 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
  8. Reduce heat and stir in heavy cream or coconut milk. Simmer for an additional 5-10 minutes.
  9. Top off with garnish after mixing everything well for one last time..
  10. Serve hot with basmati rice or naan.
  11. Enjoy your Chicken Tikka Masala!
