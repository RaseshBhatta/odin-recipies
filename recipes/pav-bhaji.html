Pav Bhaji

Description

Pav Bhaji is a popular Indian street food that originated in Mumbai and has become a favorite across the country. It features a spicy, buttery vegetable mash (bhaji) made by simmering potatoes, tomatoes, peas, onions, and bell peppers with a blend of aromatic spices called pav bhaji masala. This flavorful mixture is served piping hot with soft, toasted bread rolls (pav) slathered in butter. Typically garnished with a dollop of butter, chopped onions, and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, Pav Bhaji is both hearty and satisfying. Originally created as a quick, affordable meal for textile workers in the 1850s, it’s now enjoyed everywhere—from roadside stalls to fine dining restaurants—as a beloved symbol of Mumbai’s culinary culture.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium-sized potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, bell peppers), boiled and mashed
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 large tomatoes, pureed
  • 2-3 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons pav bhaji masala (store-bought or homemade)
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 tablespoons butter (plus more for serving)
  • Cilantro leaves, chopped (for garnish)
  • Pav (bread rolls)
  • Chopped onions and lemon wedges (for serving)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil 2 large potatoes, 1 cup cauliflower, ½ cup green peas, and 1 chopped carrot until soft.
  2. In a large pan, heat 4 tablespoons of butter and add 1 chopped onion. Sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add 2-3 chopped green chilies and 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste. Sauté for a minute.
  4. Add the pureed tomatoes and cook until the oil separates.
  5. Add the boiled and mashed vegetables, 2 tablespoons pav bhaji masala, and salt to taste. Mix well.
  6. Add water to adjust the consistency and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  7. In a separate pan, toast the pav (bread rolls) with butter until golden brown.
  8. Serve the bhaji hot, topped with a dollop of butter, chopped onions, and cilantro. Serve with toasted pav and lemon wedges on the side.
  9. Enjoy your delicious Pav Bhaji!
