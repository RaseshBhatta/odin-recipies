Pizza

Description

Pizza is a universally loved dish that originated in Italy and has evolved into countless regional and international variations. At its core, pizza consists of a dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a wide variety of toppings—from classic pepperoni and mushrooms to more creative choices like pineapple, arugula, or even truffle oil. Whether baked in a wood-fired oven or delivered to your doorstep, pizza is celebrated for its versatility and comfort. It brings people together at parties, game nights, and casual dinners, and its endless topping combinations make it easy to tailor to individual tastes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) active dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup warm water (110°F or 45°C)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your choice of toppings (pepperoni, bell peppers, onions, mushrooms, etc.)

Cooking Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, combine warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for about 5-10 minutes until frothy.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour and salt. Make a well in the center and add the yeast mixture and olive oil.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for about 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  6. Punch down the risen dough and roll it out on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  7. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  8. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  9. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  10. Add your choice of toppings.
  11. Bake in the preheated oven for about 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  12. Remove from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, slice, and serve hot.
  13. Enjoy your homemade pizza!
